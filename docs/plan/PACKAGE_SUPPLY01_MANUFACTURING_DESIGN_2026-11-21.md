# Design Note — `SUPPLY-01` Slice 2: Manufacturing BOMs, Work Orders, QC, Lot Output/Returns & `STOCK-01` Cost Conservation (2026-11-21)

Aligned with `docs/adr/0039-supply-chain-wms-3pl-manufacturing.md`, `docs/plan/PACKAGE_SUPPLY01_PLAN_2026-11-21.md`, and Master Plan §10, §13 Stage 6, §14.1.1 (`SUPPLY-01`: «Товар другого собственника недоступен; выпуск/возврат сохраняют партии, движения и себестоимость»).

## Key Decisions
1. **Seven branch-scoped tables in `0139_supply_manufacturing_and_costing.sql`** (invoker rights only, `require_enabled_module('supply')`, tenant RLS, restrictive branch RLS `-- Supply manufacturing branch scope:` at EOF):
   - `supply_boms` & `supply_bom_components`: versioned Bills of Materials for an `output_item_id` with component items, per-batch quantities, scrap allowance (`scrap_bps`), and standard labor/overhead minor-unit costs.
   - `supply_work_orders`: production work orders bound to a `company_entity` goods owner, tracking `issued_material_cost_minor`, `returned_material_cost_minor`, `labor_cost_minor`, `overhead_cost_minor`, `completed_quantity`, `scrap_quantity`, and `output_total_cost_minor`.
   - `supply_work_order_issues`: component lot issues to a work order. Deducts `supply_lot_balances` for the component lot & bin, posts a real `STOCK-01` `write_off` (`reason='used'`) via `stock.post_document`, reads the exact moving-average valuation deducted in `gba.stock_valuations` (`cost_minor`), and records a `supply_wms_movements` (`movement_kind='production_issue'`) row.
   - `supply_work_order_returns`: unused component returns from a work order back to a warehouse bin. Posts a real `STOCK-01` `return` (`reason='unused'`) against the component issue's `STOCK-01` `write_off` line, reads the exact returned valuation from `gba.stock_valuations` (`cost_returned_minor`), restores the exact component `lot_id` balance in `supply_lot_balances`, records a `supply_wms_movements` (`movement_kind='production_return'`) row, and reduces net material cost on the work order.
   - `supply_work_order_qc`: quality-control inspection record (`passed_quantity`, `scrap_quantity`) gating completion output.
   - `supply_work_order_outputs`: finished-goods output. Creates or updates the output lot in `supply_lots` with exact `unit_cost_minor`, computes `total_cost_minor = (issued_material_cost_minor - returned_material_cost_minor) + labor_cost_minor + overhead_cost_minor`, posts a real `STOCK-01` `receipt` (`reason='purchase'`) for `total_cost_minor` so `gba.stock_balances`, `gba.stock_costs`, and `gba.journal_entries` reflect the manufactured finished goods, credits `supply_lot_balances`, and records a `supply_wms_movements` (`movement_kind='production_output'`) row.
2. **Goods-Owner Isolation in Production**:
   - Work orders require a `company_entity` goods owner, and composite foreign keys + triggers prevent issuing any component lot belonging to a different goods owner (such as a `counterparty_3pl` bailor).
